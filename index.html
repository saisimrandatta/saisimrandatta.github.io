writing

Epidemic-Response Vaccine Platforms: A Landscape Analysis
MSc dissertation research, UCL · 2026
A comparative decision framework for filovirus outbreak response, scoring six vaccine platforms — live-attenuated, inactivated whole-virus, protein subunit, virus-like particle, mRNA and viral-vector — against scalable manufacturability, immunological efficacy, stability, analytical complexity and deployment logistics. No platform dominates on all five: selection is an explicit trade-off set by the epidemiological, logistical and regulatory profile of the outbreak in question. mRNA's design speed, for instance, is offset by cold-chain and booster-tracking constraints in resource-limited settings.
